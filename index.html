Sentinel

Pre-launch · 66/66 tests

"Building Trust Through Evidence" — EU AI Act compliance

An evidence-oriented compliance assessment platform for AI system providers who must demonstrate conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — validated by 4 independent experts and tested end-to-end.

ChallengeThe EU AI Act requires article-by-article proof of conformity, under fines of up to €30M. It's usually done manually, with no verifiable link to the actual code.
ArchitectureAn 18-phase pipeline analyzes the code and documentation, extracts evidence, and maps it to articles, sealed with a real RFC 3161 timestamp.
🔍18-Phase Analysis

Pipeline across source code, config, and documentation.

🔐RFC 3161 Seal

Timestamp verified with OpenSSL, not simulated.

🧭Audit Navigator

Structured browsing of findings by article and risk tier.

✍️Live Signatures

Digital signing with live content-change verification.

Guanina

Live · real transactions

Flower marketplace — also known as „Piața de Flori"

Built on Next.js Edge Runtime with Cloudflare D1, KV, and R2, integrating Stripe Connect for split payments and a QR-based pickup system. Runs on 47 incremental schema migrations applied without downtime.

ChallengeA shared multi-vendor platform must guarantee strict data isolation — one seller must never be able to query or view another seller's orders, inventory, or settlements.
ArchitectureEvery seller gets a fully isolated dashboard, enforced at the schema and query level. Payments route directly to the seller via Stripe Connect, with commission withheld automatically at the payment layer.
⚡Edge Architecture

Cloudflare D1, KV & R2 with no dedicated server.

SmartDrive

Live · paying customers

The voice copilot for rideshare drivers

A native Android app that reads the rideshare screen via local OCR, runs a real-time profit-calculation engine, and delivers the result by voice — backed by cloud sync and a full internal operations dashboard.

ChallengeRideshare apps only surface the gross fare. Computing true net profit — fuel, wear, commission — in real time, without touching the driver's account or its data, requires reading the UI externally and safely.
ArchitectureRuns passively alongside the rideshare app, using on-device OCR to read fare offers and a local calculation engine to compute net profit, delivered through text-to-speech — no access to the driver's rideshare account at any point.
🎙️Local OCR Engine

🔊Voice-First Mode

💰Real-Time Profit Engine

FAQ

What you want to know

What is MOXO Technology? +

MOXO Technology designs and builds complete full-stack applications — a live marketplace with real payments, a mobile copilot with paying subscribers, a compliance platform tested end-to-end, a price-tracking app. Every product is documented with verifiable evidence.

What is Sentinel and what regulation does it cover? +

Sentinel is a compliance platform for the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). It analyzes source code and documentation to generate evidence mapped to the regulation's articles, tested with 66 end-to-end tests and validated by 4 independent experts.

What is Guanina (Piața de Flori)? +

Guanina is a live marketplace where florists list bouquets with limited stock, customers pay via Stripe Connect and pick up their order with a QR ticket. It runs on 47 production migrations and passed a publicly documented security audit.

What is SmartDrive? +

SmartDrive is a live native Android app with paying subscribers that calculates the real-time net profit of every rideshare trip and announces by voice whether it's worth accepting.

What is Deal Hunter RO? +

Deal Hunter RO is a mobile app in development for iOS and Android that automatically tracks prices across Romanian online stores and sends push notifications when prices drop below your threshold.
